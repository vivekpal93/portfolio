A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Curious by nature. Building as I learn.

I am a Computer Science Engineering student at Rajkiya Engineering College (REC) Mainpuri in Uttar Pradesh, India.

My path in technology isn't defined by buzzwords or exaggerated titles—it's driven by genuine curiosity about how software works under the hood. I enjoy breaking down algorithms, understanding how web browsers render content, and writing code that solves real-world challenges.

I believe that the best way to master computer science is by building practical projects, understanding core principles, and continuously refining one's craft through iteration.

Outside of traditional engineering coursework, I have a deep appreciation for creative storytelling, video editing, and digital media production. I find that creative discipline sharpens my problem-solving ability and helps me design software that feels thoughtful and human.
